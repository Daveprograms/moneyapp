# Daily Profit 💰

A clean, modern daily money tracker matching your exact preferred layout, featuring bright/dark mode, natural language text entry, and instant screenshot scanning.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Exact Layout & Clean Aesthetic**:
   - **Today View**:
     - Big bold profit hero card (`+$129`) with the split green/red ratio bar.
     - Side-by-side **+ Add income** and **– Add expense** buttons.
     - **"Just tell me what happened"**: Natural language input (e.g. *"made 185 on delivery, spent 42 on gas and 14 on lunch"*).
     - **"Today's activity"**: Clean list with circular badges, descriptions, and amounts.
   - **Week View**:
     - Total profit, Made & Spent totals.
     - 3 stats: **Per day**, **Best day**, and **Kept %**.
     - **Profit by day** 7-day bar chart (green above baseline, red below).
     - Day by day breakdown.
   - **Month View**:
     - Total profit over the last 30 days.
     - 30-day visual bar chart.
     - Day by day list.
   - **Goal View**:
     - Track progress towards your savings target.
     - One-click deposit and editable target amounts.

2. **☀️ Bright Mode & 🌙 Dark Mode**:
   - One-click toggle in the top-right header to switch seamlessly between crisp Bright mode and sleek Dark mode.

3. **📷 Screenshot Scanner (OCR)**:
   - Click **Scan Screenshot / Receipt**.
   - Upload any screenshot from a delivery app (Uber, DoorDash, Instacart), banking app, or photo of a receipt.
   - The app reads the numbers and text using on-device OCR, figures out whether it's money made or spent, and lets you confirm and add it in one tap!
